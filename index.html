04 / About

Make the About section tell one connected story.

The current About has useful material: 15+ years of experience, the company’s evolution, service lines, named industry partners, and a people-centred leadership philosophy. Its main weakness is that these points sit beside one another rather than building toward a memorable thesis.

Current About, summarized from your profile

A strategic business leader and entrepreneur with over 15 years across business development, humanitarian operations, and industrial services in the UK and several West African countries. As CEO of Roy Global Limited, you describe the company’s evolution from a family-run transport enterprise into a mining services company focused on drilling, blasting, explosives management, fleet operations, and logistics. You emphasise safety, innovation, environmental stewardship, team empowerment, partnerships, inclusive growth, and sustainability.
What to improve: open with your current sector and role, explain what Roy Global does, then connect operational standards with local workforce development. Keep the humanitarian dimension, but integrate it as part of your leadership philosophy rather than a separate label.

Your new About section

I lead Roy Global Limited, a Sierra Leonean mining services company supporting operations through drilling and blasting, explosives management, and logistics.

My work sits at the intersection of operational performance and long-term local capability. Mining support depends on more than equipment and execution. It also depends on safety discipline, reliable partnerships, skilled people, and systems that can perform consistently in demanding environments.

Over the course of my career, I have worked across business development, industrial services, and humanitarian operations in the UK and parts of West Africa. That experience has shaped how I approach leadership: build strong teams, invest in capacity, and align commercial goals with the wider communities in which a business operates.

At Roy Global, we are focused on delivering dependable mining support while strengthening local expertise and creating opportunities for Sierra Leonean talent. I believe companies in our region should be able to compete to high standards while contributing to sustainable economic development.

I value conversations with mining operators, industry partners, investors, and people working to build stronger industrial capability across Sierra Leone and West Africa.

For company information and our services, visit https://royglobalsl.com/
